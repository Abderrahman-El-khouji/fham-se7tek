
# fham-se7tek
> Making health information simple, structured, and accessible for everyone.

# About:
Free nonprofit educational platform that helps people understand their health through clear, simplified, and organized information.

#Our Goal:

To become a trusted starting point for anyone who wants to understand a health topic.

A place where people can think:

> "I have a health question. I can start by understanding it here."

Not to replace healthcare professionals, but to help people become more informed and aware of their own health.

#MVP Features :

The first version focuses on one core feature:

 📚 Simplified Health Articles

Organized articles covering important health topics, including:

- What is this topic?
- Why does it matter?
- Common risks and causes
- Prevention strategies
- Healthy habits
- When to seek professional medical advice

#Project Principles :

### Simple First

Medical information should be understandable without requiring medical knowledge.

### Reliable Information

Health content should be based on trustworthy medical sources.

### Accessible For Everyone

A teenager, an adult, and an elderly person should all be able to understand the same content.

### Education, Not Diagnosis

فهم صحتك provides educational information and does not replace consultation with healthcare professionals.

#Disclaimer :

فهم صحتك provides health education information only.

It does not diagnose, treat, or replace advice from qualified healthcare professionals.

If you have symptoms or health concerns, consult a medical professional.
